How this works
Each slot now produces three files. Fill in the fields below and watch the homepage-banner preview update as you type, then download all three: News1.txt (short version — feeds the homepage banner and the notification bell), News1.png (the banner image), and an index.html (the full article page people land on when they click "Learn More" or a notification). Upload the .txt and .png straight into assets/news/ on your server, same names, overwriting what's there. Upload the index.html into a folder named /news/1/ (or /news/2/, /news/3/ — matching the slot), creating that folder if it doesn't exist yet. Your edits here are just a local drafting tool — nothing goes live until you upload all the files.
📦 Download Everything as One ZIP
Packages all three slots' current drafts at once — assets/news/News1.txt, News1.png, News2.txt, News2.png, news3.txt, news3.png, plus news/1/index.html, news/2/index.html, news/3/index.html — matching the exact folder layout of the live site, so you can unzip and drop it straight in. Also includes a flat, conveniently-named copy of each article (e.g. Season 2 In Production.html) for your own reference. Only slots with a headline, short description, full description, link, and image filled in are included.
